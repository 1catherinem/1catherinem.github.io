A comic that follows a part-time model and barista, Eris, and one of her customers, Willow. Eris really likes Willow after talking to her for the first time, but unbeknownst to her, Willow had already been stalking her for months- following her at night, watching her through her windows, and had even broke into her house to install cameras. She is completely obsessed with her, and would do anything to make her be with her. Including making a deal with the god of emails to put her in a time loop so she can find a way to ensure Eris will stay with her forever.
