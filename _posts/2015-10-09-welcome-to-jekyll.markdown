At age 19, Dzogbeve Vosa Avi discovers she'll be forced into servitude as atonement for an ancient sin her father's ancestors committed. Rather than embracing this fact, she fights her fate, and finds herself as the champion of the dying moon goddess, Masa. Masa promises liberate her and others like her, if she frees her from her mortal chains by killing the seven brides of her godchildren including her only friend and "Second self" Dzoame Kudzo Diagbe, a half-god forced into marriage with her godly father.
